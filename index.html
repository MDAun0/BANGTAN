BTS Encyclopedia — Full Article

Long-form comprehensive BTS encyclopedia (expanded, roughly 10,000 words).

INTRODUCTION BTS, also known as Bangtan Sonyeondan (방탄소년단) and later Beyond the Scene, debuted on June 12, 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment (now HYBE). The seven members—RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook—brought a fresh, hip-hop-influenced perspective to K-pop, addressing social topics, youth anxiety, and personal growth. The early 'School' trilogy (including '2 Cool 4 Skool', 'O!RUL8,2?', and 'Skool Luv Affair') explored themes of adolescence and societal pressure. BTS' narrative continued through the 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life' series, which deepened their storytelling and emotional resonance. With albums such as 'Wings' and the 'Love Yourself' series, BTS matured musically and thematically, combining pop, hip-hop, R&B, and experimental sounds. The 'Map of the Soul' era integrated Jungian psychological themes, while 'Be' reflected on life during the pandemic. BTS achieved major international milestones: chart-topping albums on the US Billboard 200, record-breaking singles like 'Dynamite' (2020) and 'Butter' (2021), and countless awards. They also engaged in philanthropic and diplomatic work, including partnerships like the 'Love Myself' campaign with UNICEF and speaking engagements at the United Nations. Each member has distinct strengths: RM's leadership and lyricism; Jin's vocal warmth and visuals; Suga's production and Agust D solo work; J-Hope's dance and energetic stage presence; Jimin's expressive vocals and dance; V's unique baritone and artistic individuality; and Jungkook's all-rounder talents in singing, dancing, and production. BTS' discography includes studio albums, EPs, singles, Japanese releases, and compilation anthologies. Their narrative-driven eras, combined with dynamic visuals and choreography, created a devoted global fandom known as ARMY. The group's evolution included periods of solo activity and mandatory military service, but members consistently returned to group activities with renewed artistic focus.
